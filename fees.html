Paid to third parties on your behalf. Leasehold fees are set by the lease and confirmed once we have it. Estimated total for most purchases: £2,500 – £5,600, excluding SDLT.

How long will it take?

Usually 4 – 8 weeks from offer accepted to completion. A cash purchase with no chain can be quicker; leasehold extensions, sitting tenants or delays in the chain can take 2 – 4 months or more, and additional charges may apply.

Key stages

  1. Taking your instructions and initial advice
  2. Checking funds and liaising with the lender's solicitors
  3. Reviewing the contract documents and carrying out searches
  4. Raising enquiries with the seller's solicitor
  5. Advising you on everything received, including the mortgage offer
  6. Signing, agreeing completion and exchanging contracts
  7. Completion, paying SDLT and registering at the Land Registry

Referral fees are occasionally paid to agents from our own fee, at no cost to you. Where we are on a developer's panel we act independently, in your interest.

03

Probate

Applying for the grant of probate, then collecting and distributing the estate.

£2,800 – £4,800+ VAT · 10 – 20 hours of partner time

Typically 6 – 12 months in total: around 8 – 12 weeks for the grant, 4 – 8 weeks to collect assets and 2 – 4 weeks to distribute them.

This estimate assumes

  • A valid will, one property and no more than two bank accounts
  • One or two beneficiaries, with no disputes or claims against the estate
  • No inheritance tax payable and no full HMRC account needed

Disbursements

  • Probate application fee: £160
  • Swearing the oath: £5 – £30 per executor
  • Bankruptcy searches: £5 per beneficiary
  • Tracked post: £10 – £20; title deeds: £3.90 each

No will, shareholdings, overseas assets or a death abroad will add cost. Selling estate property is not included.

04

Employment tribunals

Bringing or defending an unfair or wrongful dismissal claim. Charged on time; guide ranges for our fees, plus VAT.

EmployeeEmployer
Simple case£5,000 – £7,500£7,500 – £12,000
Medium complexity£7,500 – £12,000£12,000 – £15,000
High complexity£12,000 – £15,000£15,000 – £20,000

Counsel for the hearing is typically £750 – £2,000 + VAT a day. Each side usually pays its own costs in the tribunal, win or lose. We do not work on a no win, no fee basis.

How long?

Settled in early conciliation: about 4 – 6 weeks. Through to a final hearing: about 6 – 10 months.

What makes a case more complex

  • Discrimination or whistleblowing allegations
  • Many witnesses or documents
  • Preliminary issues, amendments or costs applications

05

Debt recovery

Business-to-business debts that are not disputed, charged at our partner hourly rates.

Expect anywhere from 12 to 52 weeks to payment, depending on how the debtor and the court respond. Court fees are set by GOV.UK.

Our fee includes

  1. Reviewing your documents and carrying out searches
  2. A letter before action
  3. Preparing and issuing the claim
  4. Applying for judgment in default if there is no response
  5. Requesting payment and advising on next steps

VAT on our fees cannot be reclaimed from the debtor. Enforcement is not included.
