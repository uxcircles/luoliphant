Open position

Assistant Solicitor / Senior Paralegal, Commercial & Residential Property

Permanent · Full time · London NW1 · Two days working from home

A mix of commercial and residential work: acquisitions and sales, lease assignments, funding and finance, acting for lenders, landlord and tenant, rent reviews, refinancing and new-build developments, for high-net-worth individuals and companies. The role includes business development in the UK and abroad, and reports to the Principal Solicitor.

You will bring

  • 2:1 law degree or equivalent, ideally with a postgraduate law degree
  • Around 2 years' PQE in commercial and residential property
  • Fluent English with strong drafting and communication skills
  • New-build and high-net-worth experience is an advantage
  • Commercial acumen, attention to detail and proven billing
  • Mandarin or Cantonese, and LEAP experience, are a bonus

Package: salary depending on experience, discretionary bonus, private health insurance.
