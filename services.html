Statutory declarations

UK and overseas

A statutory declaration is a written statement declared true before an authorised witness such as a solicitor. We prepare it to your specification and, if needed, arrange for it to be sworn.

How we can help

  • Change of name on a passport
  • Identity, nationality or marital status
  • Company and director declarations, including solvency
  • Immigration applications
  • Oaths, declarations and affidavits
