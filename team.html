Our firm

The global destination for UK legal expertise.

Lu Oliphant is a boutique London firm with a global reach. Since 1999 we have helped thousands of overseas investors do business in the UK.

Above and beyond, case by case.

Our approach combines high performance with a personal touch. We know from experience that every client's situation is unique, so we look at every aspect and its implications for you, anticipate potential issues and reduce risk, now and in the long term.

We are accredited under the Law Society's Conveyancing Quality Scheme, and many of our clients are private and corporate clients from the Far East.

  • High performance

    Efficient, well-run matters, with risks anticipated rather than discovered.

  • A personal touch

    A small team, so the partner you meet is the partner on your file.

  • Cultural fluency

    We understand how clients from China and Hong Kong like to work, and explain UK law accordingly.

In the community

The Chinese Health & Wellbeing Clinic

Lu Oliphant is a charitable key sponsor of the Chinese Health & Wellbeing Clinic, which aims to improve access to healthcare for the British Chinese community.

Experienced NHS doctors and therapists fluent in Mandarin, Cantonese and English run physical and mental health clinics and health workshops at the Chinese Community Centre in Chinatown, on Sundays from 11am to 4pm. Many older people and first-generation migrants cannot use new online services, so a face-to-face clinic helps them overcome language and cultural barriers.

A partnership of the London Chinese Community Centre, UK Chinese Medical Services and Catalyst Therapy Services. Chinese Community Centre, 2 Leicester Court, London WC2H 7DW · 020 7439 3822.
