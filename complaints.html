Complaints

If something goes wrong, tell us.

We are committed to a high-quality legal service. Telling us when we fall short helps us improve.

How to complain

Contact us as soon as you are aware of the problem. Write to:

Mr James Lau
Lu Oliphant Solicitors LLP
210 Euston Road
London NW1 2DA

or email info@luoliphant.com. We have eight weeks to resolve your complaint. If we change any timescale we will tell you why.

  1. Within 5 days

    We acknowledge your complaint in writing and send you a copy of this procedure.

  2. Investigation

    Our client care partner, Mr James Lau, reviews your file and speaks to the person who acted for you.

  3. Within 14 days

    Mr Lau invites you to a meeting to discuss and, we hope, resolve the complaint. He confirms what was agreed in writing within 3 days of the meeting.

  4. Within 21 days

    If a meeting isn't wanted or possible, he sends a detailed written reply with his proposals instead.

  5. Review

    Still not satisfied? Tell us why. Another partner may review the decision, and we confirm our final position within 14 days.

If we can't resolve it: the Legal Ombudsman

The Legal Ombudsman looks at complaints independently. You must complain to them within six months of our final response, and no more than one year from the problem, or from when you should reasonably have known about it.

legalombudsman.org.uk

Concerns about our behaviour: the SRA

The Solicitors Regulation Authority can help if you are concerned about dishonesty, the handling of your money, or unfair treatment because of age, disability or another characteristic.
